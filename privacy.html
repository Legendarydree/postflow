Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 8, 2026

Postflow is a local desktop application that can connect to supported third-party services through OAuth. This policy explains how information may be processed when using Postflow and this public website.

Information processed

When a user connects a supported third-party account, Postflow may process account identifiers, authorization codes, access tokens, refresh tokens, granted permissions, and content-related information required to provide the features the user explicitly requests.

How information is used

Information obtained through third-party integrations is used only to authenticate the connected account and perform actions authorized by the user. Postflow does not sell personal data.

Third-party platforms

Connected platforms operate under their own privacy policies and terms. Users should review the policies of each third-party service they choose to connect.

Public website

This static website is informational and does not intentionally collect account credentials or OAuth tokens.

Security

Users should keep their computer, browser session, and connected accounts secure. Sensitive credentials should never be published in source code or public repositories.

Data deletion and revocation

Users may revoke Postflow's access through the connected platform's account settings where supported. Local application data may also be removed by deleting the relevant Postflow configuration from the user's device.

Changes

This policy may be updated as Postflow evolves or as platform requirements change.
